FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before you start uploading.

Which file types are supported?
NexaDocs supports PDF (.pdf) and PowerPoint (.pptx, .ppt) files. Each file can be up to 50 MB. Support for additional formats including Word documents is on our roadmap.
Can I upload PowerPoint files?
Yes. Both .pptx and the older .ppt format are fully supported. Viewers can open presentations directly in the browser with slide navigation — no PowerPoint license required.
How do viewers access files?
Viewers log in to their NexaDocs account and see files shared with them on their dashboard. Files can also be shared via a direct link. You control who gets access — by individual, by course group, or via an open link.
Is my data secure?
All files are encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. We do not share your data with third parties. Files are stored across geographically redundant servers for 99.9% availability.
Is there a storage limit?
Free accounts include 10 GB of storage. Institution accounts can request expanded storage. We'll notify you well before you approach your limit and give you options to manage space.
